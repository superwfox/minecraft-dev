## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: one original pixel-art profile-picture avatar derived from the user's existing default guardian beast icon. Input image 1 is the PRIMARY IDENTITY AND PIXEL-STYLE REFERENCE, the approved default-icon-pixel-v1.png. Create a Grokbot-like avatar interpretation while STRICTLY RETAINING THE REFERENCE'S VISIBLE PIXEL-ART CHARACTER. User-confirmed requirements: black pixel capsule eyes AND KEEP THE ORIGINAL OPEN MOUTH, FANGS, TEETH AND MUZZLE. Preserve the white/light-gray/darker-gray monochrome palette, pointed lion ears, recognizable ornamental curling mane, central forehead spiral, nose, lower mane tuft and frontal head composition. The transformation is primarily in the eyes: give the beast two solid BLACK VERTICAL CAPSULE EYES with deliberate stair-stepped pixel corners, no iris, no pupil or eye whites inside the black shapes. The two capsule eyes should be clearly separated, balanced and fully visible, against light white/silver-gray facial areas; leave enough light pixels around each capsule that they do not merge into the dark eyebrow or eye-socket shadows. Simplify only the immediately surrounding eye/brow pixels as needed to make the simple bot expression legible. Keep the existing fierce playful wide-open mouth and its visible fangs and teeth; do NOT erase the mouth or make a blank mouthless bot face. Borrow Grok Bot's minimal expressive eye language without replacing the guardian beast with a generic human-faced robot. Style: authentic polished retro-game sprite, approximately 64x68 logical pixel construction, coherent chunky square pixels, crisp step-shaped contours, hard-edged grayscale shading clusters, around 8 discrete gray tones. Preserve the original ornate-but-readable pixel mane and silhouette. The result must visibly look pixelated even at normal avatar viewing size. No smooth flat vector curves, antialiasing, blur, continuous gradients, 3D rendering, shiny chrome, photorealism, or detailed robotic panels. No new antennas, mechanical ears, robot body, crown, gems, accessories, extra decorative objects, text, letters or logos. Composition: one front-facing head only on a square 1:1 canvas, head occupying roughly 76-80 percent of the canvas, generous even transparent margins so the ears and mane stay inside a circular profile-picture crop. Fully transparent alpha background outside the silhouette, no tile, circular badge background, vignette, checkerboard, drop shadow, floating sparkles, frame or watermark. Output one standalone transparent PNG avatar, aim for 1024x1024.
```
